# `<Kind>  # <decision>`, one per kind. A decision starts with one of:
#   translated: <module and how>     the addon writes this line in Japanese
#   english: <reason>                the line stays as the client wrote it, and why
# tests/python/test_tooltip_line_kinds.py fails when an inventoried kind has no decision or a decision names
# a kind the client does not have. Re-check every decision when a new kind appears on a client build.
