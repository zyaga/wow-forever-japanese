# ADR-011: Provenance layers: relabel the "CraftJapanizer" import tag, source complete translations from their originals, reject truncated text, encode paragraphs

- **Status:** Proposed; accepted once the in-game check of a multi-paragraph trusted quest (for example 26 or 27) shows its paragraphs
- **Date:** 2026-09-13

## Context
The Classic plugin's quest corpus (and therefore `data/`) tagged 4,727 quest entries `Translator = "CraftJapanizer"`. The lineage runs QuestJapanizer (lalha, 2008, wiki-backed) → CraftJapanizer (midoridge, 2010–2012) → WoWJapanizer (milai, 2012–2022) → Classic Quest Japanese Translator (2024). Tracing it showed the tag is neither a translator nor CraftJapanizer's own data pack. It is the label WoWJapanizer's author put on a bulk import of the QuestJapanizer wiki that kept only the text before the first paragraph separator. 868 of the layer's 1,645 trusted descriptions were first-paragraph-only (quest 456: one paragraph where the English has three; quest 1452: `ヒック!` for a 508-character English). Its titles, objectives and completions, all one-paragraph fields, were complete. The alignment check cannot see truncation: paragraph one aligns.

The complete human texts survive in public releases: QuestJapanizer 0.5.8 (CurseForge 15442 / file 395901, 2009) bundles 1,402 wiki-flagged rows with full text and per-row `翻訳:` credits; CraftJapanizer_Quest 2012031300 (CurseForge 23293 / file 580134) carries 4,953 rows with a named translator. The wiki itself (seesaawiki.jp/w/questjapanizer, entries to 2013) is still online.

The Japanese corpus carried no paragraph-break character: WoWJapanizer / CraftJapanizer rows encode a break as two spaces, QuestJapanizer as four.

Two constraints frame the decision. A truncated translation never ships: the player sees the client's English, with no marker. And the layer is kept, with only the bad descriptions removed, because dropping the whole layer would also remove about 1,000 complete titles, objectives and completions.

## Decision
1. **Relabel, don't drop.** `import predecessor --relabel-translator CraftJapanizer=questjapanizer-wiki`: the tag becomes the honest attribution to the wiki community (the same label given to uncredited QuestJapanizer rows). Nothing is deleted at import; the completeness rule decides per field.
2. **Completeness is part of "trusted".** `wfj check` rejects a variant as `truncated:<ja paragraphs>/<en paragraphs>` when the English has two or more paragraphs, the Japanese one, and the Japanese is under 0.22 of the English length, or when the Japanese is under 0.08 of the English length regardless. Calibrated on the store at the time: 859 of the 868 known first-paragraph-only descriptions flagged (99 %), 0 of the 397 named-translator descriptions. **Structural rule:** a variant from a layer that is first-paragraph-only *by construction* (the relabelled `cqjt` + `questjapanizer-wiki` layer, `paragraphs.FIRST_PARAGRAPH_ONLY_LAYERS`) is truncated whenever the English has two or more paragraphs and the Japanese one, whatever the ratio. A long first paragraph is still a first paragraph: QuestJapanizer's own wiki rows mirror the English paragraph count 99.3 % of the time, so a one-paragraph copy of a multi-paragraph English is almost never whole. Recorded as check `length` whenever the English is multi-paragraph or the tiny-ratio cut fired.
3. **Import the originals as first-class sources.** QuestJapanizer-2009 `wiki` rows (`source qjp@0.5.8`; translator = the row's `翻訳:` credit, else `questjapanizer-wiki`) and CraftJapanizer_Quest-2012 rows with a named translator and status `1` (`source cjq@2012031300`). Machine rows (QuestJapanizer `auto` from Livedoor MT, CraftJapanizer unnamed rows from excite MT or unattributed) are never imported. New sources **merge** into the store: identical text → `origins` (and a named credit on the incoming copy replaces the community label on the kept line; 952 lines gained a person's name this way); different text → a conflict variant with its own provenance. A trailing `翻訳:<name>` credit is stripped from every prose field (its forms: `翻訳:Az`, `翻訳： Pepper Pot`, `翻訳　Forsaken`, `[翻訳：Narks]`); the Description credit names the translator, a credit on another field is the fallback; values that still contain 翻訳 are counted at import.
4. **Tie-break by completeness, then source, then attribution.** When several variants pass the rules, the one covering more of the English paragraphs wins; at equal coverage the newest source (`cqjt` > `cjq` > `qjp` > `qjwiki`); within a source a named translator beats the community label. Recorded as check `tiebreak` (never when a `ruling` decided). Only a same-rank disagreement waits for a `ruling`. Completeness comes first because, with source first, 195 descriptions would ship the one-paragraph wiki copy while a complete 2009 variant sat in `conflicts`. A passing `correction` ranks ahead of all three keys ([ADR-012](012-human-decisions-survive-regeneration.md)).
5. **One paragraph encoding.** At import, a run of two or more spaces after a line-closing character (`。！？!?」』）)` and the corpus' `>` `＞` `…` `.`) becomes `\n\n` for every quest source; `generate` escapes it; the client's `SetText` renders it. English paragraph counts come from pfQuest's `$B` markers.
6. **The wiki is not fetched.** Seesaa Wiki's guideline assigns all copyright in wiki content to the wiki's administrator (「編集者は…編集したコンテンツに関する一切の著作権…が当該ウィキの管理者または譲受者に譲渡されることを承諾する」), grants Seesaa a promotional licence, and grants no reuse licence to third parties. It prohibits programs that overload the servers or post automatically (「弊社の設置するサーバなどに過負荷を与えるプログラムなどの設置および行為」, 「自動的に…コンテンツ投稿…を行うなどのプログラム」) but says nothing about reading. Redistribution in a GPL addon is therefore the administrator's call, not ours: the page **parser** is built and fixture-tested (against a synthetic page), the **fetcher is not**. The 2009 zip is that same administrator's own redistribution and is used.

## Consequences
- Every trusted quest description is complete by rule; a future source cannot introduce a partial text.
- Descriptions and objectives rejected as `truncated` with no complete variant in the two files show in English until completed. They are completed by a machine re-translation of the whole line ([ADR-023](023-server-only-text-drafted-in-measured-batches.md)), with the truncated text kept as a ruled `reject` variant. No truncated description is left in the store.
- `data/` gains `\n\n` inside `ja`, the `truncated` reason, the `length` / `tiebreak` checks, and the `qjp@` / `cjq@` sources: string-level, no schema bump.
- `translator` names a person or a community, never an addon.
- Ties resolved by priority are visible (`tiebreak`) and reversible with a `ruling`.

## Alternatives considered
- **Drop the CraftJapanizer layer**: measured, it would remove 978 descriptions and 1,067 titles, a thousand complete titles, objectives and completions lost for no gain in correctness. Rejected.
- **Ship truncated text with a marker**: ruled out. The only allowed markers are the stale and missing markers ([principle 3](../architecture/principles.md#3-japanese-by-default-english-one-key-away)), and a partial translation is wrong, not stale.
- **Scrape the wiki**: the terms leave redistribution rights with the administrator, and the zips already complete 473 formerly first-paragraph-only descriptions with clear provenance. Not done without permission.
- **Keep spaces as the paragraph encoding**: invisible in-game and ambiguous with double-spaced sentences.
