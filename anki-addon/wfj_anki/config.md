**WoW Forever Japanese** has its own settings window: Tools → WoW Forever Japanese → Settings.

- `folder`: your World of Warcraft folder. Empty: the usual places are searched.
- `front`: `sentence` (the whole sentence, with the word marked) or `word` (the word alone).
- `audio`: `back` (the sentence's audio plays with the answer) or `front` (with the question too).
